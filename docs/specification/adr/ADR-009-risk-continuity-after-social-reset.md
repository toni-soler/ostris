# ADR-009 — Risk continuity after social reset

Status: Accepted — 2026-08-24, Phase 3 continuity semantics

## Context

People may legitimately start a new social profile, while profile replacement must not automatically erase defaults, confirmed fraud or sanctions.

## Decision candidate

Public/social history may reset. A restricted community-scoped RiskSubject may link verified profiles/accounts and retain minimal lawful risk events. It is private, purpose-limited, retention-controlled, audited and appealable; no global blacklist or portable history exists in v0.1.

## Consequences

Only CONFIRMED continuity automatically applies private history; CONTESTED causes review/prevention, not sanction. Production retention/lawful basis still requires legal/DPIA review without blocking wire interoperability.
