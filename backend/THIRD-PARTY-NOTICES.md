# Third-party notices

This Apache-2.0 reference implementation uses unmodified third-party runtime libraries under their respective licenses.

- Spring Boot, Spring Framework/Security/Data, java-json-canonicalization and Flyway Community: Apache-2.0.
- PostgreSQL JDBC: BSD-2-Clause.
- IDAX Core: project dependency under the IDAX distribution terms. Its runtime tree includes JasperReports, OpenPDF and JFreeChart under LGPL/MPL-compatible distribution terms; upstream license notices must accompany any binary distribution that packages them.
- Jackson, Hibernate, HikariCP, Tomcat and Micrometer: permissive or Apache-family licenses declared upstream.

JUnit, Mockito and Testcontainers are test-only and are not included in the runtime distribution. No third-party source is copied or vendored. This is an engineering compatibility review, not legal advice; a production distribution must retain every packaged dependency's license notices.
